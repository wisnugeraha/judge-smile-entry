# Make the Judge Smile

One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today.
The warmest, most surprising line takes the $5.

This repository is a program/v1 entry: `main.py` writes one text to
`/output/entry.txt`, then the house judge compares entries two at a time on the
published brief (subject, warmth, surprise, one concrete detail, fresh wording).

## Entry

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`. The line (188 chars):

> My mum called just to say the tomatoes finally came in. ‘They took their time,’ she said, still out in the garden. I bit one on the balcony and called her back, and she let the phone ring.

## Why this line

A phone call from a parent, just to share something small. The surprise is the
last beat: she let the phone ring, because she was still out there. One concrete
detail (the tomatoes, the balcony) and no greeting-card phrasing.
